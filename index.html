About the Doctor

Dr. Aalisha Kanodia

BDS, MDS (Conservative Dentistry & Endodontics)
Chief Dental Surgeon | Endodontist & Smile Designer

Dr. Aalisha Kanodia completed her Bachelor of Dental Surgery (BDS) from the prestigious Manipal College of Dental Sciences, Manipal, in 2015. A distinguished academic achiever, she was awarded the Gold Medal by both Manipal College of Dental Sciences and Manipal University for her outstanding performance.
Following the completion of her one-year mandatory clinical internship in 2016, Dr. Kanodia secured an impressive All India Rank of 101 in the All India Postgraduate Dental Entrance Examination, earning admission to the esteemed Government Dental College, Guwahati, to pursue her Master of Dental Surgery (MDS) in Conservative Dentistry and Endodontics.
During her postgraduate training, she emerged as a University Topper under Srimanta Sankaradeva University of Health Sciences, Assam, reflecting her unwavering commitment to academic excellence and clinical proficiency.
Driven by her passion for restorative and aesthetic dentistry, Dr. Kanodia has undergone advanced training in Microendodontics, Smile Designing, and Full Mouth Rehabilitation. Her areas of expertise include root canal treatments, complex restorative procedures, smile makeovers, and comprehensive rehabilitation of worn and compromised dentitions.
Known for her meticulous attention to detail and patient-centric approach, Dr. Kanodia strives to blend science, artistry, and precision to deliver predictable, long-lasting, and naturally beautiful results. She is also a visiting Endodontist and Smile Designer at several reputed dental clinics across Guwahati, where she is recognised for her expertise in managing complex endodontic and aesthetic cases.
After a decade of dedicated clinical practice and enriching experiences across various facets of dentistry, Dr. Aalisha Kanodia has finally brought her vision to life by creating ENSA Dental Care—a thoughtfully designed space that embodies excellence, ethics, comfort, and personalised care.
At ENSA Dental Care, her mission is to provide exceptional, evidence-based dentistry while creating an experience that instils comfort, confidence, and trust in every patient.
From subtle smile enhancements to full-mouth rehabilitation, every plan is crafted to feel comfortable, transparent and beautifully tailored to you.
